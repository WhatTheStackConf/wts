# Feedback surface

Mode: Operate. Audience: invited WTS attendees completing a short survey on a phone or laptop, often from an email, in mixed lighting. The parent session loaded Impeccable context and owns final browser capture and independent finish review.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A short invitation-only conversation, not an account workflow. One required answer is explicit; optional detail never gates completion.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit WTS dark tinted neutrals, Space Grotesk, restrained magenta action, cyan focus and amber recovery. No display effects, nested cards, tracking widgets or decoration.

STORY: Understand privacy and the actual closing date, rate the experience, optionally explain improvements or a session, submit once, receive a focused acknowledgement.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Narrow single-column content; strong left-aligned heading, compact introduction, privacy disclosure and deadline precede a native vertically stacked overall radio group. Submit is at the form end, never floating over answers.

FORM: User-approved narrow questionnaire; no direction tournament or seed applies. Sections use whitespace and fine separators. Session feedback stays visible and explicitly supports multiple reviews before final submission. Selected reviews form a persistent titled list with rating/comment summaries, Edit / Done editing and Remove controls. The bottom picker and Add another session action exclude duplicates. Editors collapse with hidden CSS, never unmount their native inputs; removing and re-adding resets only that review. All answers remain local until Submit feedback; no post-submission editing is promised. Exact retry retains the same answers, and success scrolls to the thank-you heading.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

No new visual world or raster assets. Existing DESIGN.md remains authoritative. Parent owns integration screenshots and independent review; focused tests cover the client boundary and form serialization.
